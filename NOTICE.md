# TongDou Notice

This notice explains which parts of the TongDou repository are covered by
the open hardware license and which parts are not.

## Hardware License

TongDou hardware design materials that are explicitly marked as open
hardware are licensed under the CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 -
Permissive (`CERN-OHL-P-2.0`).

The full license text is provided in the root `LICENSE` file.

The hardware license applies only to materials that are explicitly marked as
covered hardware source, such as future released schematics, PCB design files,
manufacturing files, mechanical design files, and original fixture design
files.

## Attribution

When using, modifying, manufacturing, or selling hardware based on TongDou
open hardware materials, please retain the original project attribution:

```text

https://github.com/solitary-dev-50/tongdou
```

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, the following materials are not licensed

- Audio assets.
- Logos.
- Character designs.
- Website content.

- Official packaging.
- Internal demo firmware.
- Private test data, credentials, service configuration, and backend settings.

All rights to these materials are reserved by their respective owners unless a
separate license is provided.

## Brand and Official Product Boundary

The `TongDou` name, logo, character identity, official media, and official
packaging are not granted under the hardware license.

Third parties may build, modify, and sell their own versions based on released
TongDou open hardware files, but they may not:

- Present third-party products as official TongDou products.
- Claim official certification or endorsement.
- Use official TongDou branding in a way that creates confusion.
- Use official media or packaging to imply official approval.

If you publish a derivative version, please make it clear that it is a
third-party version based on the TongDou open-source hardware project.
